FundAxis Account Recovery

Forgot Your Password?

For security reasons, FundAxis password recovery is handled through authorized support channels.

Security notice

Passwords cannot be recovered or displayed. Authorized support personnel can only reset account access according to FundAxis security procedures.

Who should you contact?

Employee / User

Contact FundAxis IT Support or your Administrator to request a temporary password reset.

After a reset, you may be required to change your password at your next sign-in.

IT Support

Contact an authorized Administrator to reset your FundAxis account password.

IT Support must not reset its own credentials through a public page.

Administrator

Contact another authorized FundAxis Administrator or an authorized system administrator for controlled account recovery.

Administrator recovery should follow the organization's emergency access procedure.
