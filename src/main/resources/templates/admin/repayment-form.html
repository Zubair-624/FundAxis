Repayment Recording Rules

  • Use the Loan Application ID and installment number to identify the correct schedule.
  • The amount must be greater than zero and cannot exceed the remaining balance.
  • The reference number must be unique.
  • A full payment makes the installment PAID; a partial payment makes it PARTIAL.
  • Financial corrections are made by reversal, not by editing.
